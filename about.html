Meet Hanju Tsai, PT, MS

Professional Background

Hanju has been a licensed physical therapist for over 25 years. She graduated from the Department of Physical Therapy at National Taiwan University in 2002 and earned her master's degree in 2006, with research focused on Kinesio taping and lymphedema treatment. She obtained her U.S. physical therapy license in 2009 and has since continued to expand her clinical knowledge and skills through professional education and hands-on experience.

Life in Los Alamos

Having lived in Los Alamos since 2007, Hanju has developed a deep appreciation for the community. Fluent in English, Mandarin Chinese, and Taiwanese, she enjoys connecting with people from diverse backgrounds.

Commitment to Excellence

Hanju is dedicated to lifelong learning and evidence-based practice. She consistently exceeds professional continuing education requirements, completing more than 30 hours of continuing education every two years and over 120 hours within the past five years. Her ongoing professional development includes specialized training in Dry Needling, shoulder rehabilitation, and the human movement system.

Beyond her clinical experience, Hanju has a strong academic and research background, with six research publications and presentations focusing on pain, lymphedema assessment and management, and functional outcomes. Her research has been published in professional journals, including the American Journal of Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation and Supportive Care in Cancer. Her academic and professional achievements have been recognized through multiple awards, reflecting her commitment to research excellence, academic achievement, and patient-centered care.

Certifications & Specialized Training

  • Dry Needling Certification – The Myofascial Institute
  • Rehabilitation of the Shoulder – MedBridge
  • The Movement System: Foundations of Body Regions – MedBridge

Honors & Awards

  • Presidential Academic Excellence Award – Received three times during her undergraduate studies (1997–2001), recognizing placement within the top 2% of her class.
  • Phi Tau Phi Scholastic Honor Society – Elected as an honorary member at National Taiwan University (2005).
  • Graduate Student Academic Research Performance Award – National Taiwan University (2005).
  • Second Place, Best Healthcare Service Award – National Taiwan University Hospital, Bei-Hu Branch (2006), selected through patient voting among hospital employees.
  • Graduate Student Outstanding Publication Award – National Taiwan University College of Medicine (2008).
