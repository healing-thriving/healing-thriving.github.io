A Movement-Based Approach to Physical Therapy

Hanju has a strong interest in integrating movement-based approaches into physical therapy. She regularly practices Pilates, weight training, belly dancing, Zumba, and yoga, and enjoys incorporating principles from these activities into her treatments, particularly in areas such as core stability, gluteal activation, postural awareness, mobility, strength, and movement control.

As a mother of three active children involved in swimming, tennis, skiing, and cross-country running, Hanju has also gained a deeper appreciation for the physical demands of different sports and activities at various stages of life. These personal experiences, combined with her clinical background, have broadened her perspective on movement, injury prevention, strength development, and functional recovery. She enjoys drawing from both her professional training and personal experiences to help patients find practical and sustainable ways to move better.

My Treatment Philosophy: Empower Solution-Focused Physical Therapy

This is a treatment philosophy developed through Hanju's clinical experience, personal movement journey, and inspiration from Solution-Focused Pastoral Counseling.

She believes that patients are the experts in their own lives and have valuable strengths, experiences, and resources to overcome their challenges. Her approach emphasizes recognizing individual strengths, exploring possibilities, and building on existing resources rather than focusing solely on problems.

By integrating movement, mind, and meaningful communication, Hanju strives to create a collaborative and empowering environment where patients feel heard, understood, and encouraged to take an active role in their recovery. Rather than simply providing treatments or exercises, she guides patients in understanding their bodies, recognizing their strengths, and discovering practical solutions that work for their individual needs and lifestyles.

She believes the ultimate goal of physical therapy is not to create long-term dependence on a therapist, but to empower patients with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to become their own physical therapists—the experts in understanding, caring for, and managing their own bodies.

Beyond Rehabilitation: Wellness & Lifelong Movement

Hanju believes that physical therapy and movement education can extend beyond treating injuries and medical conditions. She is passionate about helping people improve their strength, mobility, posture, movement efficiency, and overall physical well-being, even when they are not experiencing pain or injury. Whether the goal is recovering from an injury, improving athletic performance, developing healthier movement habits, or maintaining an active lifestyle, she enjoys helping people discover their potential and continue to grow stronger and healthier.

The Meaning Behind Healing & Thriving

The name Healing & Thriving reflects Hanju's belief that true well-being involves more than physical recovery. It represents her vision of integrating physical health, mind, and meaningful communication in the journey toward better health.

Healing is not simply about reducing pain or recovering from an injury; it is also about understanding oneself, building confidence, and discovering the ability to move forward. Thriving means continuing to grow, move, and live well beyond recovery.

Through an individualized, whole-person approach, Hanju hopes to help people not only heal, but also thrive—becoming more independent, resilient, and confident in caring for their own well-being.
